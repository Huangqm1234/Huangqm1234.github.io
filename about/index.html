Hi! I am a master's student studying robotics at the University of Pennsylvania, originally from Beijing, China. My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics and machine learning, with a focus on human-centered robot learning. I am particularly interested in:

  • Learning from Humans — acquiring skills through demonstrations and feedback.
  • Learning to Understand Humans — interpreting human intentions, preferences, and needs.
  • Learning with Humans — enabling adaptive collaboration through interaction and clarification.
  • Learning to Help Humans — developing reliable and intelligent robots that can meaningfully assist people in the real world.
